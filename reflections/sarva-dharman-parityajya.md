# Sarva-dharmān parityajya

| moola | Gita | Ramanuja (Sharanagati Gadya) | Alwar |
|-------|------|------------------------------|-------|
| Sarva-dharmān parityajya | Abandon means, not action | Abandon self-qualification | Nammalwar: I tried knowing, I tried serving, I tried loving — nothing worked. You alone entered and claimed me |
| Mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja | Exclusive refuge | Explicit choice: tvat pāda mūlam eva | Periyalwar: Pallāṇḍu pallāṇḍu… I sing only so that You remain |
| Ahaṁ tvāṁ mokṣayiṣyāmi | Krishna takes over responsibility | rakṣa rakṣa — You protect | Kulasekhara Alvar: I have no discipline, no devotion, no knowledge — yet You still claim me |
